Over the past several years, I've been researching a simple question:

Can long-term investing become easier by following pure price action instead of trying to predict the market?

After studying hundreds of charts, I discovered that many high-quality companies follow a remarkably simple trend structure. Rather than searching for the perfect bottom, my strategy focuses on:

  • Following the primary trend.
  • Entering only after price confirms strength.
  • Defining risk before every investment.
  • Letting winners run while controlling losses.
  • Removing emotions through a simple, rule-based approach.

This strategy isn't based on complicated indicators or market predictions. It simply answers two important questions before every investment:

• Where should I enter?
• Where should I exit if I'm wrong?

Once these rules are defined, the rest of the process becomes much simpler.
