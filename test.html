📈 200 EMA ka Use Kaise Karein

Simple Language Mein — Beginner Friendly Guide

#Trading#EMA200#TechnicalAnalysis

200 EMA (Exponential Moving Average) ek bahut important indicator hai jo trend ka direction batata hai. Isko use karna bahut simple hai.

1. Trend Pehchano

  • Price 200 EMA ke upar hai → market UPTREND (bullish) — Buy zone.
  • Price 200 EMA ke neeche hai → market DOWNTREND (bearish) — Sell/Skip zone.

2. Support / Resistance (Laxman Rekha)

200 EMA aksar strong support ya resistance ki tarah kaam karta hai. Price jab 200 EMA ko touch karta hai toh wahan se bounce back ka chance hota hai. Isliye isko "Laxman Rekha" bhi kehte hain — dono charts (TCS aur Redington) mein dikhta hai.

TCS — 200 EMA (Laxman Rekha) pe bounce + Stop Loss

3. Entry Rule (Pullback)

  • Buy: jab candle 200 EMA ko upar se touch karke wapas upar jaaye.
  • Sell: jab neeche se touch karke wapas neeche jaaye.

4. Stop Loss

Entry candle ke neeche ya 200 EMA ke thoda niche SL rakhein — jaise TCS chart mein "STOP LOSS" mark kiya gaya hai.

5. Trendline Breakout + 200 EMA

Jab price trendline ke saath 200 EMA ko bhi tod ke upar jaye, toh strong breakout confirm hota hai. Redington chart mein dekho.

Redington — Trendline Breakout + 200 EMA ke upar

Golden Rule: Kabhi bhi 200 EMA ke against trade mat lo. Trend apna dushman nahi, dost hai. 🙏
