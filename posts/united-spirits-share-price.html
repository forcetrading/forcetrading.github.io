On July 2, 2026, I shared United Spirits Ltd. with members of my Telegram investment community.

At the time, the stock was trading around ₹1,375. There was no excitement. No major headlines. Just a clean weekly chart forming a familiar breakout structure. That's exactly what caught my attention.

The Original Setup

The chart had three characteristics I like to see:

  • A healthy long-term trend
  • Higher lows holding firmly
  • A descending trendline ready for a potential breakout

Rather than predicting the future, I simply shared the setup and waited for price confirmation.

📊 Shared Before the Move
The charts were shared inside our Telegram community on July 2, 2026 – before the breakout became obvious. That's the advantage of following chart structure instead of chasing momentum.

The Result So Far

Since the original post, United Spirits has moved from approximately ₹1,375 to ₹1,463. The breakout has been confirmed, and the stock has started trending higher.

Why I Continue Using Weekly Charts

Weekly charts help remove short-term market noise. Instead of reacting to daily price swings, they allow me to focus on trend, structure, and price action. I've seen similar breakout structures appear in many successful stocks over the years. Different companies. Different sectors. The same type of setup. That's why I continue building my watchlist around weekly chart patterns.
