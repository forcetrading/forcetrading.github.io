ITC Monthly Chart: Is ₹270 the Level That Could Decide the Next Big Move?

Sometimes the best opportunities appear when a stock reaches long-term support, not when it's making new highs.

📅 July 2026 🕐 5 min read 📈 ITC Ltd.

After a prolonged correction, ITC is approaching a very interesting zone on the monthly chart. The ₹270 support area has become an important level to watch. According to the FTY Indicator Framework, this is a region where buyers could start showing interest if price continues to respect the long-term trend.

🔍 Why ₹270 Matters

Looking at the monthly chart, several things stand out. This isn't about making bold predictions — it's about recognising a structurally significant zone where the odds of a reaction increase.

Key Support Zone
₹270
Major historical support — a level where buyers have previously stepped in
  • Price is approaching a major historical support zone around ₹270. This isn't a random number — it's an area that has acted as both resistance and support in previous cycles.
  • The long-term trend indicator is still below price, suggesting the broader structure hasn't completely broken down. The FTY framework places significant weight on whether the primary trend remains intact.
  • Previous reactions around similar areas have attracted buying interest. Historical behaviour doesn't guarantee future outcomes, but it does provide a data-backed context for decision-making.

Rather than predicting the exact bottom, I prefer waiting to see how price reacts at important support levels. The chart will always give the final answer.

🎯 Breakout Target
₹500

If price holds ₹270 and a strong bounce materialises, the monthly chart structure points towards a long‑term target near ₹500. This is not a forecast, but the logical measured move based on the pattern and historical resistance levels.

👀 What I'm Watching Next

The coming weeks will be critical. Here are the two primary scenarios I'm tracking, along with the logic behind each:

🟢
Buyers Defend ₹270
If buyers absorb supply and price holds this zone, it could become the foundation for the next meaningful move towards ₹500. Confirmation via a higher-low structure on the weekly chart would strengthen the case.
🔴
Support Fails
If ₹270 breaks with conviction on strong volume, patience becomes equally important. The FTY framework emphasises waiting for a new base to form before reassessing the trend.

Good investing isn't about guessing. It's about reacting to what the chart confirms. The ₹270 level is a decision point, not a prediction. I'll be watching how price behaves here — not what I hope it does.
